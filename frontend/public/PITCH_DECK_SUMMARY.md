## Objection Handling

### Objection 1: "Netflix will just build this themselves"
**Response:** 
"Netflix won't build cross-platform discovery - it hurts their walled garden strategy. We're Switzerland, neutral across all platforms. Plus, we move faster (MVP in 8 weeks vs Netflix's 18-month product cycles)."

### Objection 2: "JustWatch already does discovery"
**Response:**
"JustWatch is a search engine. We're a social network. They have traffic; we'll have community. Users come to JustWatch once a month; they'll come to us daily because of gamification and crew chat."

### Objection 3: "User acquisition will be too expensive"
**Response:**
"Our model has built-in virality: referral program, leaderboards (FOMO), crew invites. We're targeting $5 blended CAC with 1.3 viral coefficient. Letterboxd grew to 10M users with minimal paid marketing - organic works in this category."

### Objection 4: "How do you make money if content is on other platforms?"
**Response:**
"We're not hosting content - we're the guide. Think Kayak for travel: they don't own hotels but make money on bookings. We earn affiliate fees when users subscribe to platforms ($5-20 per signup), plus premium subscriptions ($4.99/month) and ads."

### Objection 5: "Can't users just use social media to find content?"
**Response:**
"They do - but then they have to manually hunt across 5 apps to find where it's streaming. We close that loop: see it's buzzing on Twitter → discover on OTT Linker → tap → watch in Netflix app. We're the missing link."
