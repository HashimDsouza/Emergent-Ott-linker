## 🎨 DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

### Core Principles:
1. **Clarity, Engagement, and Ease of Discovery**
2. **Minimize Cognitive Load** - Distinct, non-overlapping icons
3. **Lean, Highly Usable** - Built for retention
4. **Mobile-First** - 90% users on mobile
5. **Clean Structure** - Intuitive navigation
6. **Instant Answers** - Where to watch? What's trending? How to engage?

### Visual Strategy:
- **Iconography:** Distinct, tappable icons with visual cues
- **Taglines/Microcopy:** Short, engaging, evocative language
- **User Focus:** Prioritize personalization & discovery
- **Clean Structure:** Uncluttered, organized
- **Floating AI:** Bottom-right corner, always accessible

## 👥 TARGET AUDIENCE INSIGHTS

### Indian OTT Viewer Profile:
- **600M+ users** juggling multiple platforms
- **Decision fatigue** from endless options
- **Sports-driven** (especially cricket/IPL)
- **Social proof** matters - trending conversations
- **Quick access** to watch history needed
- **Conversational AI** adoption increasing
- **Diverse languages** - regional support critical
- **Gamification responsive** - quizzes, polls, points
- **Daily checks** for new releases & pop culture

### Pain Points to Address:
- ❌ Overwhelmed by clutter
- ❌ Can't find content across multiple apps
- ❌ Disconnected from social/cultural trends
- ❌ Complex or hidden rewards
- ❌ Stale/repetitive content

## 📱 MOBILE-FIRST REQUIREMENTS

### Size Guidelines (to be applied):
- **Header height:** Compact (~40-50px mobile)
- **Icons:** 20-24px mobile → 28-32px desktop
- **Text:** 10-12px mobile → 14-16px desktop
- **Spacing:** Tight on mobile (gap-2) → Comfortable on desktop (gap-4)
- **Bottom nav:** Fixed, always visible

### Responsive Behavior:
- **Mobile (<768px):** Show essential icons only, compact spacing
- **Desktop (≥768px):** Show all icons, comfortable spacing
- **Overflow:** Hamburger menu for secondary items on mobile
